# UX review — the office worker's day (2026-10-13)

A Chrome walk-through of the app as `office_test` (office role), done as a
user rather than a tester: take an order, get it picked, dispatch it, bill
it, record the money, and look at every screen the office reaches from the
sidebar on the way. Window 1280 px wide, the laptop size the office uses.

The happy path works end to end and reads well: create → Confirm → Auto
allocate → weights → Close short → Loaded → Mark dispatched → invoice issued
→ Email invoice / Record a payment. Dialogs name their action, the invoice
correctly bills the short line at €0, the payment form's "Auto-fill oldest
first" is exactly what a shop paying a lump sum needs, and the 404 page is a
page. What follows is what got in the way, ranked by how much it costs the
person doing the job. Each item says what was seen, why it matters, and the
smallest change that would fix it. Items already on `SMALL_FIXES.md` are
marked *(known)*.

## A. Traps in the flow

1. **The order form takes lines there is no stock for, silently.** The
   picker shows the price (`Organic Milk · 1L Bottle (€2.50)`) but not free
   stock, and the quantity box gives no hint. 24 × 1L went through with zero
   sellable milk; the first warning was "No available stock" after Confirm,
   by which time the order was on the floor's queue. The run-sheet editor
   has the same gap (its stock footer shows "—" for milk, see F2).
   *Fix:* put free stock in the option label or a live hint beside quantity
   ("295 available"), and an amber line on the form when a quantity exceeds
   it. The data is already one query (`BatchItem::freeStockByVariant()`).

2. **Dashboard tells the office to allocate an order that can't be
   allocated.** A `pending` order entered from the run sheet appears under
   "Unallocated · 2 need action" with an "Allocate →" button, but allocation
   refuses pending orders ("Confirm them first") and the Stock allocation
   worklist excludes them, so the dashboard says 2 and the worklist says 1.
   *Fix:* label pending rows "Confirm →" (landing on the order page's
   Confirm button) or count them separately ("1 to confirm · 1 to allocate").

3. **Nothing says a delivery date has passed.** ORD-20260710-001 (confirmed,
   deliver 14 Jul) sits in every queue three months on: dashboard, Stock
   allocation, and `/picking` opens on "Tue 14 Jul" as its default day
   because that is the nearest day with work. The office has no cue that
   the order is dead rather than due.
   *Fix:* an "overdue delivery" tag wherever delivery date is printed, a
   dashboard line ("1 order past its delivery date — cancel or re-date"),
   and the picking queue should prefer today over a past day.

4. **The invoice can't be sent from where it was issued.** After Mark
   dispatched the flash says "Invoice INV-000001 issued — send it from
   Finance", and the order's Invoice row reads "INV-000001 · Unpaid · not
   yet sent" with no send action. The Invoices list likewise has no row
   actions: to send one you open it. Two extra hops for the most common
   finance task.
   *Fix:* an "Email invoice" button on the order's billing line while the
   invoice is unsent, and Send / PDF actions on the Invoices list rows (or
   a "Send all not yet emailed" button over the "Not yet emailed" tile).

5. **Online-order noise on every login.** The dashboard carries a warning
   banner ("Online order sync has not completed successfully yet · View
   log"), the greeting line says "Online sync is behind", and the Online
   Orders page says "Not configured" — all because Mossorders isn't set up.
   An office worker reads a persistent amber banner as something wrong.
   *Fix:* when `MOSSORDERS_BASE_URL` is empty, hide the banner and the
   greeting clause, and either hide the nav item or label it "not set up".

## B. Layout at 1280 px

6. **The product page overflows horizontally.** On `/products/{product}`
   the document is 1323 px wide in a 1280 px window; the right column's
   buttons are clipped ("Add varian", "Dele", "New batc", "Activ") and a
   horizontal scrollbar appears. The master pane (`320px`) plus the
   sidebar leaves the detail pane too narrow for its tables.
   *Fix:* let the variant and batch tables shrink (drop the Unit column,
   abbreviate headers) or collapse the sibling pane below `xl`.

7. **The order header breaks once the action bar fills up.** On a `ready`
   order the row holds Docket · Loaded onto van · Edit · "Tick loaded onto
   van first"; once loaded it adds Mark dispatched. The order number then
   wraps to two or three lines and the subtitle to three. Measured: the
   `h1` is two lines on a dispatched order at 1280 px.
   *Fix:* put the actions on their own row under the title, and keep
   "Tick loaded onto van first" as the disabled Mark dispatched button's
   tooltip rather than a grey sentence in the bar.

8. **Weight entry is squeezed into a scrolling table.** The cheese line's
   allocation table scrolls sideways; Pick · Fulfil · #1 · #2 · Total ·
   Remove are stacked inside the ACTION column. Four columns of numbers the
   picker never reads (Production, Allocated, Fulfilled, Weight) take the
   width the inputs need. *(The per-unit input count is a known item;
   this is about the column it lives in.)*
   *Fix:* render the weight inputs as a row beneath the allocation, full
   width, with the running total beside the Fulfil button.

9. **Dashboard order queue wraps everything.** Order numbers break over
   three lines and customer names over four; the items summary under the
   customer name is what squeezes them.
   *Fix:* drop the item summary to a title attribute or a second line
   spanning the row, and give ORDER `white-space: nowrap`.

10. **Small wraps.** "Issue all 1" on Awaiting invoice becomes a two-line
    pill. The run-sheet editor's "+ Ref" toggle wraps as "+ / Ref".

## C. Words and states

11. **Grammar and age labels on the dashboard.** "1 order need allocation";
    ages read "3mos before" / "13h before". The age is measured from
    `order_date` (midnight), so an order created five minutes earlier
    showed "13h before".
    *Fix:* "1 order needs allocation"; "3 months ago"; measure from
    `created_at`, or better show the delivery date, which is what the
    office sorts by.

12. **Two names for each state.** The sidebar tag says "Preparing", the
    stepper says "Picking"; tiles say "Ready to ship … packed" while the
    status is "Ready"; lines say "Needs fulfilment" while the button says
    "Fulfil" and the floor says "pick".
    *Fix:* one word per state everywhere: Pending · Confirmed · Picking ·
    Ready · Dispatched · Delivered, and "pick/picked" on lines.

13. **"Credit limit €0.00" means no limit but reads as no credit.** Shown
    twice on the customer page (Business terms and the Account panel) and
    the form gives no hint that 0 means unlimited.
    *Fix:* print "No limit" when 0; add "0 = no limit" under the input.

14. **Three labels for one figure.** The customer page shows Outstanding
    (Business terms), Open invoices and Net owed, all €161.60.
    *Fix:* keep Net owed in the Account panel; drop Outstanding from
    Business terms.

15. **Batch cards contradict themselves.** Every expired card is tagged
    "Active" and "Expired" together and says "Ready: Now"; the product
    page's Recent batches table does the same and its TOTAL QTY column
    prints just "kg". And "Can't delete — 5 records depend on it" is
    printed on every card next to View · Edit although nobody is deleting.
    *Fix:* "Expired" replaces "Active"/"Ready now" on the card; move the
    delete reason to the batch page or a tooltip; fill or drop TOTAL QTY.

16. **Placeholders shipped as features.** "More actions coming soon." on
    the batch page; "Activity log not yet enabled" and "Low-stock tracking
    isn't configured yet — set a threshold per product variant" on the
    dashboard, with no place to set one. The office reads these as broken.
    *Fix:* remove the panels until the feature exists, or link to what
    configures them.

17. **Short-close copy over-promises** *(known)*. The dialog says "24 units
    will be released back to stock" and the flash repeats "24 units
    released back to stock" when nothing was reserved. The stepper's "0 of
    26 picked" also counts the no-stock line *(known)*.

18. **Weights prefilled but not summed.** The per-unit boxes show 5.000
    (nominal) while "Total: 0.000 kg" until each is edited. Either
    prefill nothing (the picker must weigh) or sum the prefill.

19. **"Assign stock in one tap" never goes away.** The banner and the
    Auto allocate button stay after auto-allocate has done all it can (the
    remaining line has no stock), so the page still looks like it wants
    something.
    *Fix:* once every line is allocated or has no stock, swap the banner
    for "Allocated — 1 line short of stock".

## D. Forms

20. **Required fields the office doesn't have produce junk data.** The
    customer form requires Email, Street, City and Postal code; the
    customer table holds postal codes "x", "c", "r", "X" and a street
    "Kilmantin Hill,," — people typed something to get past validation.
    Email is required even though Invoice delivery offers "Print" / "no
    invoice".
    *Fix:* make Postal code and City optional, make Email required only
    when Invoice delivery is Email, and strip trailing commas.

21. **Form defaults don't match the customers.** Payment terms default to
    Immediate while every customer on file is Net 30; Credit limit is a
    required "0.00".
    *Fix:* default to Net 30 (or the most common value); make credit
    limit optional, blank = no limit.

22. **Delivery date auto-fills to today without saying why.** Picking
    Barnhill at 13:00 set delivery to 13/10, the day's run date, after the
    van had probably gone.
    *Fix:* a hint under the field ("Tuesday · Dublin Tue run") and, when
    the run day is today, default to next week's date.

23. **Delivery address looks mandatory.** A large unlabelled textarea
    beside Order date; nothing says it overrides the customer's address.
    *Fix:* "Leave blank to deliver to the customer's address" and a
    smaller box.

24. **Weight-priced line totals are estimates but don't say so** on the
    create form (€160.00 for 2 wheels; the order page later says
    "~ 10.000 kg"). *Fix:* "~€160.00 · est. 10 kg" on the form.

25. **Order list.** Filters need a Filter click (no auto-apply), there is
    no search by order number or customer reference, no date range, the
    "Docket" link sits inside the STATUS column, and cancelled orders show
    Payment "Pending".
    *Fix:* auto-submit on change, a search box, hide payment for
    cancelled, move Docket to the actions column.

26. **Customers list.** A red "Delete" link on every row for customers
    without orders; search needs a Filter click.
    *Fix:* delete lives on the customer page behind a dialog (cycle 12,
    parked); auto-submit the search.

## E. Controls that do nothing or shouldn't be there

27. **The notifications bell is inert.** Clicking it does nothing.
    Remove it until it has content.

28. **Profile → Delete account is offered to an office user.** In a
    closed system where admin creates users, self-deletion is a trap, and
    the copy ("all of its resources and data will be permanently deleted")
    is alarming beside orders and invoices.
    *Fix:* admin-only, or remove.

29. **Finance has two navigations.** The sidebar's Finance group (5
    items) and the page tab bar (8 items: + Consolidated, Sent, Export)
    disagree; Sent and Export are only reachable from the tabs.
    *Fix:* one set, or add the missing three to the sidebar.

30. **Delete links on every product and variant** in the catalogue
    *(cycle 12, parked)*.

## F. The run sheet

31. **The summary strip declares the day done at 13:00.** "ALLOCATED 1/1
    · All stops covered — ready to pick", "LOADED 1/1", "DISPATCHED 1/1 ·
    All stops dispatched" while 14 of 15 stops have no order. The
    denominators count ordered stops only, which is correct and
    misleading.
    *Fix:* "1 of 1 ordered stops · 14 without an order" and the "All stops
    …" sentence only when every stop on the run has an order.

32. **"—" in the stock footer means two things.** For milk it means zero
    stock; for cheese crates it means not applicable.
    *Fix:* print 0 (red when the column has orders) for stock, keep "—"
    for n/a.

33. **Capacity is an amber banner.** "Capacity: 1600 kg" in warning
    colour reads as a problem. Make it a tag in the run header.

34. **Every row prints the full address** including the junk postcodes
    from D20. The driver has the route; the office needs the name. Show
    the town only, full address on hover.
