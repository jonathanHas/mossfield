# UX review part 2 — production, the floor, the van, admin, and finance off the happy path (2026-10-28)

A Chrome walk-through of `http://mossfield.local` as a member of staff, taking
the parts the 2026-10-13 review did not: production and stock as the office,
the picking flow as the factory on a phone, the driver, the admin screens, and
finance beyond invoice-and-pay. Where this review met something the previous
one already found, it is cited by number (`2026-10-13 #n`) rather than
re-described; items already on `SMALL_FIXES.md` / `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` are
marked *(known)*.

A great deal works, and works carefully. The batch form refuses more bottled
litres than raw milk and asks "yes, really" above the ceiling; the cut form
refuses too many packs and a pre-production date with sentences a person can
act on; maturing hold, release and undo round-trip cleanly; a write-off and
its reversal leave an honest history row; and `/stock` agreed to the unit and
the euro with everything done during the session (the €28,052 total value
recomputes by hand). On the phone the picker gets one tap for a fixed line, a
weight box per wheel with a comma turned into a point and a tenfold weight
refused ("Check the decimal point"), a single box for bulk packs, undo, mark
short and reopen. Every office-only URL gives the factory a polite 403 page,
every bad id a proper 404, and the role matrix held everywhere it was
probed. Finance did the right thing at each fork: dispatch issued a per-order
invoice and correctly held a weekly customer back for the consolidated run,
the preview burnt no number, the credit note reduced the balance, void put the
order back in the queue, a payment left its surplus on account and "Apply
credit" spent it oldest-first, the statement ledger ran to the right closing
balance, and the CSV came out with the right columns. No screen overflowed
horizontally at 1280 px or 390 px (measured headless, `scrollWidth` against
`clientWidth`, 54 URLs × 2 widths).

What follows is what got in the way, ranked by what it costs the person doing
the job.

## Environment facts (not findings)

- **Accounts.** The dev database had no `admin` user and the `factory_test`
  / `driver_test` passwords had drifted, so `AdminUserSeeder` was run: it
  created `admin` and reset the three `*_test` accounts to the documented
  password. Jon's own accounts (`jon`, `Bibi`, `lorraine`) are untouched.
- **No Mature product** existed in the dev database, so the release step of
  Mature Conversion refused (finding 10). It was then created through the
  Products screens as the office would (product 7, one Whole Wheel variant).
- **Mail never leaves this box.** `.env` still has the Laravel placeholder
  from-address, and Symfony's mailer refuses it before the log driver sees
  the message ("does not comply with addr-spec of RFC 2822"). Every send in
  this session therefore *failed* rather than being logged. The app reported
  each failure honestly (order page "Failed · Resend", invoice email history
  names the reason), so nothing below counts it as a bug.
- **Random 500s from PCRE.** PHP 8.5 under this box's Apache (prefork) cannot
  allocate PCRE JIT memory; Laravel turns the one-off warning into an
  exception, so a request occasionally 500s (13 in the log during this
  session — once on an order page, once on the consolidated preview) and the
  PDF and CSV download routes 500 *every* time, because the warning fires in
  the content-disposition code. Retrying got the CSV; the invoice PDF was
  rendered from the same template on the CLI instead and its figures match
  the order page line for line (€25.00 / €164.80 / €27.00 / €0.00 not
  supplied / €216.80). Fix on the box, not in the app: `pcre.jit=0` in the
  Apache `php.ini`, or allow the JIT its memory. Production is nginx + FPM
  and may not be affected; worth one check there.
- **Phone width.** The Chrome tool would not shrink the window below 500 px,
  so the factory flow was driven at 500 px (same Tailwind breakpoint band as
  390) and the 390 px overflow figures come from headless Chrome.
- **One clean-up.** The self-demotion test (finding 2) left the seeded
  `admin` as an office user; its role was put back with tinker so the admin
  screens could be finished. Nothing else was repaired.

## A. Things that lose data or lock people out

1. **The backup leaves every finance table out, while promising "a full
   snapshot".** *(broken)* As admin, `/backup`. The page says "Download a
   full snapshot of the database … every business table", and lists 16
   tables (159 rows). None of `invoices`, `invoice_lines`, `payments`,
   `payment_allocations`, `credit_notes`, `credit_note_lines`, `statements`,
   `document_emails` or `document_sequences` is in `BackupService::TABLES`,
   and restore deletes and reloads only the listed tables. Expected: the
   backup the page describes. Why it matters: a restore on the same box
   reloads `orders` with `invoice_id`s pointing at invoices the restore never
   touched (and vice versa after a void); a restore on a fresh install loses
   every invoice, payment and credit note ever issued, and the sequences
   restart at 1 so the next invoice collides with a number already sent to a
   customer. Smallest fix: add the nine tables to `TABLES` in foreign-key
   order with `ADDED_LATER => true`, and until then say on the page what is
   not covered.

2. **An admin can demote or deactivate *themselves* and is dropped on the
   login page without a word.** *(broken)* As admin, `/users/8/edit`, role
   Administrator → Office, Save. The save went through, the session was
   invalidated (correctly, by `logOutEverywhere`), and the browser landed on
   `/login` with no flash and no explanation. The users list already hides
   Deactivate and Delete on the "(you)" row, so the intent exists; the edit
   form has no such guard. Expected: either refuse ("You can't change your
   own role or active flag — ask another admin") or confirm and explain
   that it will log you out. The last-admin guard could not be exercised
   without touching Jon's three admin accounts, so it was not.

3. **Browser Back after a save, then submit again, writes a second
   write-off.** *(broken)* As office, `/batches/23`: write off 1 × 250g tub,
   press Back, click Write off again. The page comes back from the browser
   cache showing the pre-save state ("200 on hand", no history row, button
   re-enabled by `submit-once`), and the second click records an identical
   second write-off (198 on hand). The payment form carries a `form_token`
   for exactly this; the write-off form does not. Expected: the second
   submit is a no-op or a refusal. Smallest fix: the same per-render token
   on the write-off form (and the cut form, which has the same shape), or
   `Cache-Control: no-store` on pages that carry state-changing forms.

4. **Session ended in another tab → a bare "419 | PAGE EXPIRED".** *(friction)*
   Log out in a second tab, then confirm a write-off reversal in the first:
   a grey Laravel default page with no link anywhere. `errors/404` and `403`
   are proper pages; 419 is not. Smallest fix: an `errors/419` view on the
   same layout ("Your session ended — log in and try again" with the login
   link), or redirect to login with a flash.

## B. Wrong or contradictory figures

5. **Undoing a release leaves a ghost Mature batch on every stock screen.**
   *(misleading)* Release 3 wheels of G030426 to mature, then "Return to
   farmhouse hold" on the mature batch. Afterwards `/batches` lists
   G030426-1 (Mossfield Mature Cheese) as "Active · Ready · Stock: 0 units",
   the farmhouse card still says "Matured into G030426-1", `/stock` shows a
   "Mossfield Mature Cheese · G030426-1 · 0 units" row and counts cheese as
   "3 batches". The office reads a mature batch that does not exist. The
   model keeps the empty batch for reuse (documented); the screens should
   not show it. Smallest fix: hide batches with 0 produced from the browser,
   stock and lineage lines (or delete the mature batch on undo when it holds
   nothing else).

6. **The mature wheel weighs 2.3 kg on the product and 5.0 kg on the batch.**
   *(misleading)* The seeded Mature Whole Wheel variant is 2.300 kg
   (`ProductSeeder`; *(known)* pricing question on `SMALL_FIXES.md`), yet the
   mature batch page shows "Unit weight 5.000 kg" and "Finished weight
   ~15.00 kg" for 3 wheels, because the converted item carries the source
   wheel's nominal weight. 5 kg is the physically right figure for a
   converted Farmhouse wheel, so the odd one is the variant. Smallest fix:
   decide which is true and make the other follow it (and say "nominal from
   the source wheel" on the batch page).

7. **The batch filters "Active" and "Ready to sell" both list expired
   batches.** *(misleading)* `/batches?status=active` returns M220626,
   M230726 and M230726-1, each tagged Expired on the card; `?maturation_status=ready`
   likewise. The DB status is `active` and only the date has passed, which is
   exactly what the office is trying to filter out. Smallest fix: treat
   "Active" as active *and* not past expiry, so an expired batch appears
   only under Expired (the card already makes this distinction, 2026-10-13
   #15).

8. **The "Sold out" filter says "Get started by creating your first
   production batch".** *(misleading)* `/batches?status=sold_out` with no
   matches shows the first-run empty state with a "Create first batch"
   button. Smallest fix: "No batches match these filters" when any filter is
   set.

9. **"Sold" on a batch counts units that were only picked.** *(misleading)*
   `/batches/22` showed "ON HAND 5,990 · SOLD 10" while the order holding
   those ten bottles was still in Picking and could be undone. Sold is
   `produced − remaining − written off`, so a pick is a sale. Smallest fix:
   label the column "Picked / sold", or split picked-not-dispatched out.

10. **The accounting CSV includes void invoices at full value after promising
    that "a column total is the period's net turnover".** *(misleading)*
    `/invoicing/export`: INV-000001 (void) appears with Net 161.60, VAT 0.00,
    Gross 161.60, status `void`. The accountant summing Net gets €161.60 too
    much. Smallest fix: emit zeros in the money columns for void rows (keep
    the row and its status), or exclude void and drop the sentence.

11. **The delivery-charge toggle is offered on a run with no charge.**
    *(misleading)* `/delivery-runs` → Show charge settings: every Dublin Tue
    stop shows "€ charge: off / on" though the run's charge is €0.00, so
    switching it on changes nothing. Smallest fix: when the run charge is 0,
    show "run has no charge" instead of the toggles.

12. **"Used" on Write-off reasons counts reversed write-offs.** *(misleading,
    small)* Damaged / dropped shows "1 used" though its only write-off was
    reversed. Fine for deciding whether a reason can be deleted (history), but
    the column reads as usage. Smallest fix: "1 (reversed)" or count active
    only.

## C. Roles that see the wrong thing

13. **The factory dashboard tells the floor to write off expired stock.**
    *(misleading)* As factory, the "EXPIRED STOCK 3 · 4,326 units · write
    off" tile links to `/batches?status=expired`, where the factory has no
    write-off control (correctly). Smallest fix: drop the "write off" call
    to action from the tile for factory, keep it for office/admin.

14. **Factory sees an Edit link on every product and gets a 403.**
    *(friction)* `/products` as factory: each card offers "View · Edit";
    Edit lands on the access-denied page. Nav gates discovery; these links
    do not. Smallest fix: `@can('update', $product)` around Edit (and the
    variant Edit/Duplicate/Delete if they are rendered for factory).

15. **The driver is told routes are not set up, while a 15-stop run with a
    named driver exists.** *(misleading)* As driver, the only screen is a
    dashboard saying "Your route manifest will appear here once delivery
    routes are set up. For now, contact the office." Every other URL is 403.
    Does what it gets make sense? As a safety baseline yes: nothing to
    break. As a tool, no: the one thing a van driver needs — today's stops
    in order with the loaded tick — already exists at `/chilled-runs`, and
    the copy blames missing data rather than a deliberate hold. Smallest
    fix now: honest copy ("The run sheet hasn't been opened to drivers yet").
    Real fix: read access to the run sheet plus `OrderPolicy::load` for the
    driver role, which is the "manifest" the message promises.

16. **Add user defaults the role to Administrator.** *(friction, risk)*
    `/users/create` opens with Administrator selected; an admin creating a
    packer who forgets the dropdown makes another admin. Smallest fix:
    default to Office (the most common), or no default.

## D. Words and states that stop you

17. **Release to mature tells the office to "run the product seeder".**
    *(misleading)* `/cheese-conversion`, Release 3 → confirm → red flash
    "Mature cheese product is not configured. Run the product seeder." The
    Release button is offered on a page that cannot complete it, and the
    remedy is developer-speak. Smallest fix: check for the destination
    product when rendering; hide Release and say "Mature cheese isn't set
    up as a product yet — add it under Products with 'Made only by
    conversion' ticked" (that checkbox exists and worked).

18. **The release flash doesn't say where the wheels went.** *(friction)*
    "Released 3 wheel(s) to mature cheese." The undo lives on the *mature*
    batch's page, which the office must find by browsing `/batches` for a
    code it has not been told (`G030426-1`, a same-date collision suffix
    that reads like a second farmhouse make). Smallest fix: link the new
    batch in the flash and label the suffix ("mature batch from G030426").

19. **A refused weight wipes the good weights with it.** *(friction)*
    Picking a wheel line on the phone: #1 = 5.1, #2 = 52 → "Each unit must
    weigh no more than 15 kg … Check the decimal point." (good) → both boxes
    come back empty and the picker retypes the 5.1 in a cold store. Smallest
    fix: repopulate `old('weights')` on the refused render.

20. **"of 2 needed · 2 in batch" beside "23 in stock".** *(misleading)* The
    pick screen clamps the stepper to the line and prints the clamp as if it
    were the batch ("10 in batch", "10 pickable (FIFO)") under a batch line
    that says 6000 in stock. Smallest fix: "10 needed · batch has 6000" and
    drop "pickable".

21. **"Next order →" sends the picker to a three-month-old order, and the
    queue's ◀ jumps from today to 14 July.** *(misleading)* After closing
    ORD-20261028-001 the celebration card offered "NEXT UP An Tairseach ·
    120 items · Tue 14 Jul"; once today's orders were dispatched `/picking`
    opened on "Tue 14 Jul" (nearest day with work). The order surfaces now
    carry the overdue tag (2026-10-13 #3, closed), but the picking day header
    and the next-up card do not, so the floor is still steered at a dead
    order. Smallest fix: the same tag on the day header and next-up card;
    better, leave past-dated confirmed orders out of "next up".

22. **Credit note form: blank VAT and Credit boxes under copy that says "set
    any line you're not crediting to zero".** *(friction)* Nothing is
    prefilled (VAT % is blank though every line was 0 %), the Issue button
    looks disabled, and the first click surfaces "Please fill in this field"
    on Reason rather than on the amounts. Smallest fix: prefill VAT % from
    the invoice line, leave amounts blank, and reword to "enter the amount
    to credit on each line".

23. **Cutting from the batch page lands you on the cutting index.**
    *(friction)* Batch page → Cut Whole Wheel → save (or Cancel, or "← Back
    to cutting") → `/cheese-cutting`, not the batch you came from. Smallest
    fix: honour a `return` parameter or redirect to the batch when the cut
    was started from it.

24. **The "Actions" panel on milk and yoghurt batch pages is an empty box.**
    *(friction)* A heading with nothing under it (2026-10-13 #16 removed the
    "coming soon" line but not the panel). Smallest fix: render the panel
    only when it has an action.

25. **Statement "Generate" leaves you where you were.** *(friction)* The
    flash says "Statement generated for Cavistons." and the page is
    unchanged; the saved snapshot is only discoverable on the Statements tab.
    Smallest fix: link it in the flash or redirect to it.

## Could be prettier

- Dialog bodies that repeat their title: "Release 3 held wheel(s) into
  mature cheese?" under "Release to mature?"; the house rule says the body
  is the consequence.
- Grammar: "The 1 unpicked unit won't be supplied … reserved for them";
  "1 units across 1 line weren't supplied"; "wheel(s)".
- `/backup` mixes friendly and raw table names ("Users", "Products" beside
  `settings`, `stock_write_offs`).
- The picking queue footer reads "Continue picking" on a day with nothing
  in progress.
- "User updated." after a role change does not mention that the user has
  been signed out everywhere, which they will notice first.
- The cut-date input has no `min`/`max` though the server knows both.
- Yoghurt has no shelf life on the product, so every yoghurt batch is
  entered as "stays sellable until sold" unless the office types a date.
  The form says so honestly; this is data (judgement), like the mature
  pricing items on `SMALL_FIXES.md`.
- The order edit page, delivery-run edit/delete, user delete and the
  customer delete (cycle 12, parked) were not exercised beyond loading.

## Not reached

- **Overdue reminder.** Nothing was overdue (every invoice is within terms or
  paid) and no screen offers a reminder on an in-terms invoice, so the
  reminder route was not exercised without editing dates.
- **Last-admin guard.** Four active admins exist; demoting or deactivating
  Jon's accounts to reach "last admin" was out of bounds.
- **Backup restore** (by instruction) and **queued email** (no worker).
- **PDFs in the browser** (environment, see above): invoice, credit note,
  statement and dispatch-confirmation downloads all 500 on this box.
- **Driver at phone width**, and the factory flow at exactly 390 px rather
  than 500 px (overflow at 390 was measured headless and is clean).
- **Online Orders** (hidden behind the flag, by decision) and Mossorders.
- Double-click was tried on the cut form only (protected); the other forms
  were submitted once.
